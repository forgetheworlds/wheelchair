### Why the current parts do not establish a $200 CAD DIY kit

The cheapest *compact* motor family (~US$41 for two) plus a more credible dual driver (~US$51) and a one-piece higher-discharge candidate pack (~US$44.50) already total **~US$136.50**, even before MCU, charger, joystick, traction rollers, actual metal mounts, fasteners, shipping or taxes. The 7S3P pack rating and compact-motor torque remain unverified, so this is **not** a selectable build.

The larger D60 motor pair adds ~US$39.20 on top. Cheaper alternatives may be possible after testing, but a single-person, verified, ready-to-use, full kit **is not yet demonstrated at C$200**.

### Supplier questions to copy and send

**Gearmotor:** What is your exact 24V brushed motor SKU with **≥2 N·m continuous output torque at approximately 320 RPM**? Please provide the continuous-rated torque-speed curve, electrical and thermal limits, rated/stall current, gear ratio, full CAD or dimensioned drawing, shaft diameter/key/coupling method, full motor+gearbox length, mass, encoder option and price/shipping for **2 units to Toronto**.

**Battery:** Please quote a fully assembled, protected 24V-class battery with sufficient discharge (request ≥20–30A continuous subject to design confirmation), adequate peak capability and reliable BMS. Provide cell chemistry/configuration, absolute charge voltage, matched charger, exact dimensions, weight, current capability, test reports, protection behavior, supplier warranty and total shipped cost for **1 unit**. Do not hand-build an uncertified pack for the intended DIY user.

**Motor controller:** Is the output appropriate for two brushed motors and 8S LFP full-charge 29.2V? Continuous current **per channel**? Does it provide closed-loop controlled deceleration, wheel-speed feedback, overcurrent/overtemp protection, watchdog response, and controllable fault-state behavior? Ask for a schematic/datasheet and shipping cost for **1 unit**.

**Roller/mounting:** Calculate traction and the strength of mounting hardware after confirming the specific tire and gearmotor. Do not treat printed TPU grip as an automatically safe drivetrain.
